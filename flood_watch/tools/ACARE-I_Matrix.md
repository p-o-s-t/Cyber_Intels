# ACARE-I Threat Actor Risk Matrix

This matrix utilizes a modified **ACARE-I** model (Activity, Capability, Access, Resources, Expertise, and Intent) to score the threat actors involved in this exercise.

## Scoring Rubric (1-5)
- **1**: Negligible / Minimal
- **2**: Low / Foundational
- **3**: Moderate / Capable
- **4**: High / Advanced
- **5**: Critical / Strategic

### Definitions
- **Activity**: Frequency of observed operations/incidents.
- **Capability**: Technical proficiency and toolset sophistication.
- **Access**: Ability to penetrate and maintain persistence in target networks.
- **Resources**: Personnel, infrastructure, and financial backing.
- **Expertise**: Ability to innovate or adapt to complex environments.
- **Intent (D)**: Desire to conduct **Disruptive** operations (Deny, Degrade, Destroy).
- **Intent (I)**: Desire for **Intelligence** collection and espionage.

### Analysis for Exercise
- **HydroPhantom** represents the highest strategic threat (State-Sponsored), closely mirroring the sophistication of **Volt Typhoon** and **Chernovite**.
- **AquaLeak** represents the highest operational noise (Hacktivist/Criminal), similar to **Cyber Avengers** and **LockBit** in terms of visible impact but with lower technical depth.
